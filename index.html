OUR CORE INNOVATION & USP

Forward Departure Search vs. Backward Deadline Discovery

Indian Railways carries 23 million daily passengers. When travelling for exams, job interviews, or medical appointments, departure time doesn't matter — reaching on time does.

❌ Traditional IRCTC Flow

Search by Departure Time (Forward)

  • Departure-centric: User specifies departure date and searches blindly forward.
  • Manual verification: Shows 10+ trains leaving throughout the day, forcing passengers to open each schedule one by one.
  • Mental arithmetic: Passengers have to manually calculate transit time from station to examination hall or interview venue.
  • Critical hazard: Evening trains arriving after the commitment are shown anyway; one calculation slip = missed career opportunity.
✅ TimeTrack Rail Flow (Our USP)

Search Backwards from Venue Deadline

  • Deadline-first: User enters 12:00 PM Venue Deadline & 45m Station-to-Venue Time.
  • Automated Backward Math: Engine determines the train must reach CSMT by 11:15 AM at latest.
  • Late Train Rejection: Evening trains (19:05, 21:05, 21:55) are automatically filtered out.
  • Color-Coded Buffer: Highlights 11010 Sinhagad (Safe • 80m spare) & 12124 Deccan Queen (Tight • 50m spare).
  • AI Synthesis: Google Gemini explains the best buffer in clear, natural language with direct IRCTC booking.
